Domain Adversarial training for RUL prediction

Here is the project repository: rul-prediction (currently under development, but feel free to check it out and give feedback). Introduction: what is Domain Adaptation? RUL (“remaining useful life”) prediction for devices is gaining increasing attention in the recent literature. One problem is that of estimating RUL in cases where there training and test data from the sensors come from two different distributions A and B: this is called Domain Adaptation (DA). This is relevant when the device is used in certain conditions (settings, temperature, pressure,…) where there is little or no available labeled data, but there is enough labeled data in some other conditions. ...
